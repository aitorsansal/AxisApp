Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Axis is a shared-expense tracker for households and groups, developed and operated by an individual developer, not a company. This policy explains what information Axis collects, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, used to sign in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and basic profile info (name, profile picture) from Google, and nothing else.
  • Profile information you choose to provide — a display name, birthday, and profile photo. All optional, editable or removable anytime from Profile in the app.
  • Ledger data — the groups, expenses, and payments you and the people you share a group with enter. This can include names of people you add to a group (including people who haven't signed up for Axis themselves, added by name only), amounts, dates, and free-text descriptions.
  • Device information — if you enable notifications, a device push token used only to deliver notifications about activity in your groups.

How we use it

Solely to operate Axis: authenticate you, show your groups and ledger to you and the people you share them with, and (if enabled) send you notifications about activity in your groups. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Who can see your data

Ledger data (expenses, payments, balances) inside a group is visible to every member of that group — that's the core function of a shared ledger. Your account email is never shown to other members; only your display name, avatar, and birthday (if you've set one) are.

Third parties we rely on to run Axis:
  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Every account's data is restricted at the database level so only people who share a group with you can see it.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google, to verify your identity.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If a group is dissolved or you leave one, the group's historical expenses and payments are retained (unlinked from the group, but still attached to the people who were actually party to them) as a record for the people involved — the same way a bank keeps a record of a closed account's past transactions.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your display name, birthday, and photo anytime from Profile.
  • Remove a phantom (not-yet-signed-up) member you added, as long as their balance is settled.
  • Leave a group, transfer, or dissolve one you created (subject to settling any balance first).
  • Request full deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us — see Contact below.

Children's privacy

Axis is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted at the database level (row-level security) so that only accounts sharing a group with you — or you yourself — can read it, enforced by the database itself rather than solely by application code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
