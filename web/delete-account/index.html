Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your Axis account and associated personal data at any time.

In the app

Open Axis, go to your Profile page, and scroll to the Danger zone section at the bottom. Tap Delete account and confirm — deletion happens immediately. If you still own a group that has other members in it, you'll be asked to transfer ownership or dissolve that group first.

No longer have the app installed?

Send an email to contact.aitorsansal@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Axis account, with the subject "Delete my Axis account". We'll confirm your identity via that email address and process the request.

Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, login credentials, and Google sign-in link, if any).
  • Your profile information — display name, birthday, and avatar photo.
  • Your device push token.
  • Any groups you created that have no other members.

What's retained

Expenses and payments you were party to inside a group you shared with other people are retained as part of that group's shared ledger history, the same way they'd remain visible to the other members after you leave a group — deleting your account does not erase other members' records of a shared transaction. Your member record is unlinked from your account (it reverts to an unclaimed, name-only entry) rather than deleted outright, so the ledger history other members rely on stays intact.

Turnaround

Deleting in the app is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days of confirming your identity.
